Start your shortlist ↗
01

Choose the category before the item

For Litbuy spreadsheet finds, narrow your search to a specific need: daily sneakers, a relaxed hoodie, a lightweight jacket or an adjustable cap. A focused shortlist is easier to compare.

02

Save the exact listing and variant

Record the product URL, color, size and seller reference. Litbuy product links are only useful when you can identify the exact option you intend to purchase.

03

Compare measurements and evidence

Check the size chart against an item you already own. Review available detail photos and quality-control images, while remembering that images alone cannot establish authenticity or guarantee quality.

04

Estimate the complete purchase cost

A Litbuy spreadsheet comparison should separate the item price from delivery, applicable fees and destination charges. Recheck stock, shipping options and return conditions before payment.

Answers before you browse

A few useful clarifications.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

The phrase typically refers to a shopping list that organizes product links and comparison notes for people researching purchases through Litbuy. This page is an independent browsing guide, not an official Litbuy spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

How do I use a Litbuy spreadsheet for shoes and clothing?

Start with a category, open the product listing, and record the link, selected size, measurements, listed price and any available product photos. Compare those details before choosing an item. The category links on this page open CAFANS.

Does a spreadsheet verify authenticity or product quality?

No. Inclusion in a spreadsheet, a brand name or an illustration does not verify authenticity, seller authorization or product quality. Check the individual listing and request suitable evidence before purchasing.

What should I include when comparing total costs?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, any applicable service and payment fees, international shipping and any taxes or duties that apply to your destination. Confirm current amounts with the relevant provider before paying.
